## Project Identity

**Project Name:** openTDC  
**Full Title:** Open Engineering Repository for the John Deere 316 Time Delay Controller  
**Classification:** Engineering Knowledge Repository  
**Current Phase:** Engineering Discovery

---

## Project Objective

To systematically understand, document, reverse engineer, validate, and preserve the engineering knowledge of the John Deere 316 Time Delay Controller (TDC) through rigorous engineering methodology.

This project serves as a model for engineering-first documentation and knowledge preservation in legacy agricultural equipment.

---

## Scope

### In Scope

- Reverse engineering of the original TDC hardware
- Firmware extraction and analysis
- Bench testing and validation of OEM behavior
- Documentation of circuit design and operation
- Measurement and characterization of electrical performance
- Engineering decision documentation
- Methodology development and refinement
- Knowledge preservation for future reference

### Out of Scope (Current Phase)

- Firmware development or modification
- PCB redesign or layout changes
- Hardware redesign or enhancement
- Production manufacturing
- Commercial distribution
- Performance optimization beyond OEM specifications

### Future Scope (Pending Phase Transition)

- Modernization of hardware design
- Firmware reimplementation
- Enhanced feature development
- Production-ready designs

## Project Constraints

### Technical Constraints

### Resource Constraints

### Methodological Constraints

- Engineering rigor over speed
- Documentation completeness over rapid iteration
- Evidence-based conclusions over assumptions

---

## Risk Management

### Technical Risks

- **Hardware damage during reverse engineering**  
  *Mitigation:* Non-destructive testing prioritized; document before disassembly

- **Incomplete firmware extraction**  
  *Mitigation:* Multiple extraction methods; verification procedures

- **Measurement errors or misinterpretation**  
  *Mitigation:* Calibrated equipment; repeated measurements; peer review

### Project Risks

- **Scope creep into implementation before understanding is complete**  
  *Mitigation:* Strict phase discipline; charter enforcement

- **Loss of engineering knowledge due to poor documentation**  
  *Mitigation:* Rigorous documentation standards; session-based workflow
